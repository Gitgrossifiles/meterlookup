## Cross-brand patterns worth knowing

- **2" has three footprints across every brand**: 10 (turbo, no strainer), 15 1/4 (compound), 17 (PD / turbo with strainer). Ultrasonics (E-Series, MACH 10, Cordonel, Octave, evoQ4 AL) are sold in all three to drop in.
- **3" / 4" / 6"**: turbo = 12 / 14 / 18, compound = 17 / 20 / 24. The exceptions are Sensus OMNI T2 (19 / 23 / 27, strainer built in) and Hersey MCT II (24 / 29 / 36 1/2).
- **Fire service assemblies** are nearly universal: 4"=33, 6"=45, 8"=53, 10"=68 (Sensus Compact FireLine, Badger FSAA, Neptune Protectus III, Hersey FM3/MFM II, Metron FSA).
- **Mag meters** are all short ISO bodies and need spools in any mechanical-meter setting.
